## 🤖 ABOUT THE BOT

**SUPREMACY_SPX** is a **premium, fully-featured WhatsApp bot** designed to give you **ultimate control, maximum protection, and endless entertainment** – all from your WhatsApp.

Whether you're a group admin needing powerful moderation tools, a content creator looking to download media, or just someone who loves fun AI chats and games, SUPREMACY_SPX has you covered.

### 💎 What Makes SUPREMACY_SPX Special?

- 🔥 **250+ Commands** – The most comprehensive command set available
- 🛡️ **35+ Protection Commands** – Unmatched security against buggers, exploits, spam, and viruses
- 🧠 **25+ AI Models** – Gemini, ChatGPT, Copilot, DeepSeek, MetaAI, and more
- 🎨 **Cyber-Tech Style Menu** – A unique, premium-looking menu that sets you apart
- 📥 **30+ Media Downloaders** – TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Twitter, YouTube, Spotify, and more
- 🎮 **15+ Games** – TicTacToe, Trivia, Truth or Dare, 8ball, Hangman, and more
- 🤖 **Auto Features** – Auto-read, auto-status, auto-react, auto-typing, auto-recording
- 🔗 **Auto-Join Group & Auto-Follow Channel** – Automatically joins your group and follows your channel
- 🌐 **Pairing Site** – Users can pair without scanning QR codes
- 📱 **Universal Deployment** – Works on Pterodactyl, Render, Railway, Heroku, VPS, Termux, Docker, and more
- 🆓 **Completely Free** – Open-source and free forever

### 🛡️ Why You Need This Bot

- **Protect your groups** from buggers, exploiters, spammers, and bots
- **Boost your group engagement** with games, fun commands, and AI chats
- **Download media** directly from social platforms
- **Automate moderation** with anti-spam, anti-link, anti-badword, and more
- **Stand out** with a unique, premium-style menu that's not copied from anyone
